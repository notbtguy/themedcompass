About us

Built by doctors who have been through it.

Founded by Dr. Kunal Shah and Dev Jain, The MedCompass is a career guidance platform built by healthcare professionals who have personally navigated the NEET-UG and medical education journey.

We help students understand their options, evaluate colleges and courses, navigate the counselling process, and make informed decisions aligned with their goals. Our guidance is transparent, ethical and evidence-based, combining historical trends, counselling experience and technology.

MedStart by The MedCompass

Our post-admission bridge program

For students who have completed counselling and are waiting to begin college. Sessions led by experienced doctors, researchers and healthcare professionals give an early introduction to medical college, research, clinical exposure and the realities of life in healthcare.

The goal: less trial and error, and a start with greater clarity, confidence and direction.
